Step 6 of 6
Block scam websites
Your phone can refuse to open known scam websites. It takes one setting.
1. Tap the big button below. It copies the address and opens Settings.
2. Find Private DNS. It is usually under Network or Connections, then More or Advanced.
3. Choose the hostname option.
4. Press and hold in the box, tap Paste, and the address is filled in. (It is security.cloudflare-dns.com.)
5. Tap Save, then come back here.
Not on yet. When you're done, come back here.
