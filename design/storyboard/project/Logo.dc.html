BRAND · FIRST LOGO IDEAS

Six logo ideas for Aegida

All use the app's teal and sunflower yellow, and are drawn from scratch. Each is shown as the app icon, at small sizes, and as the single-colour icon Android uses in the notification bar. No red, to stay clear of scam pop-ups.
IDEA 1
Sunflower shield
Aegida
A shield with her sunflower at the centre. Ties to the safety picture she chooses.
Trade-off: Clear 'protection' meaning; the petals blur at 24 px.
App icon, small sizes,
notification icon
IDEA 2
Letter A shield
Aegida
A bold A with a small sun above, inside a shield. Reads as the first letter of the name.
Trade-off: Simple and sturdy; looks like many security apps.
App icon, small sizes,
notification icon
IDEA 3
Cradled sun
Aegida
A sun held in a cupped bowl. Care and keeping something precious safe.
Trade-off: Warm and unlike other security logos; less obviously 'security'.
App icon, small sizes,
notification icon
IDEA 4
Keyhole sunflower
Aegida
A round badge with a sunflower whose centre is a keyhole. Warmth plus lock-and-key security.
Trade-off: Distinctive and friendly; the keyhole is small at notification size.

notification icon

Porch-light arch
Aegida
