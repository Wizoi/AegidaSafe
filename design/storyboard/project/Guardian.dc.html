SCENARIO 9 · WHAT THE GUARDIAN HEARS

Guardian messages, retries and changing the guardian

Every message is written in her name (title: her first name), carries no link and no message text, and is sent only if she said yes to alerts.
A. WHAT A GUARDIAN RECEIVES, AND WHEN
1

Sent once when she says yes to alerts (setup or My settings) and again for a new guardian. If it cannot be sent, the daily check tries again.
2
Has not checked in
SAM'S PHONE · NTFY
ntfy · her private topic
Ruth
Aegida hasn't checked in on Ruth's phone for 2 days. The phone may be off or asleep. Please call her.
A dead-man's switch. After every daily check Aegida schedules this message for 2 days later, replacing the one waiting. While her phone checks in daily it never arrives. If the phone is off or asleep for 2 days, it is delivered.
3
One-day reminder
SAM'S PHONE · NTFY
ntfy · her private topic
Ruth
Ruth hasn't dealt with a warning from Aegida yet. It has been a day. Please call her.
A warning still not dealt with after 24 hours reminds the same guardian once. The warning stays on Home meanwhile.
Repeat back-off
A repeat of the same kind of warning still warns her every time.
Her guardian is messaged again only after 1 day, then 2, then 4, then 7 days since the last message about that kind.
Urgent problems are never held back.
Switching alerts off cancels the waiting 'hasn't checked in' message and sends nothing more.
5
Could not be sent: kept
WHAT AEGIDA DOES, NOT WHAT SHE SEES
The message is kept in a small outbox on the phone and retried at the next check, with growing waits. She is still warned right away. History says 'Aegida could not reach your guardian.'.
After many tries the line reads 'Aegida could not reach your guardian, and gave up.'.
Offline, killed mid-way, or the guardian's service down: nothing is lost.
