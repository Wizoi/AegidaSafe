FOR REVIEW · OCT 7, 2026 · MOM'S GALAXY S23+ (ANDROID 16)

Aegida

Storyboard and user scenarios for version 1
WHO USES IT
Mom
80+, retired. Doesn't reliably follow prompts or warnings. Sees one calm screen: green when all is well, one big button when something needs her.
WHO GUARDS
Guardians
Kevin, plus an optional second such as a sibling. Get short automatic alerts on their phones through ntfy. Nothing for them to approve.
WHAT IT DOES
The Aegida app
Remembers how the phone should be set up, hides fake warnings, checks apps and links against public threat lists, and walks Mom through each fix.
THE SCENARIOS (ON THE CANVAS TO THE RIGHT)
0

Mom picks her guardians, a safety picture, and emails them the install and alert links. Auto Blocker recommended, website filter on, today's setup saved.

Home screen, a history of what Aegida did, and settings where she can change her guardians and choices.

ON EVERY SCREEN
One action per screen. Steps already done are skipped.
Her safety picture and "Aegida" on every real screen.
Calm colors only. Big text. Plain sentences.
Aegida opens each Settings page and checks the result itself.
"Call a guardian" is always there.
One rule: it never asks for money, passwords, or codes.
HOW IT WORKS
Install: from the Google Play closed test track. Mom sets herself up and chooses her guardians.
Strict where it counts: default apps, safety switches, remote-control apps and scam-list matches. Normal new apps pass if Play Protect and the threat lists are clean.
On her phone: heads-up banners. No full-screen takeovers, no overlays.
Guardians are told automatically because she opted in during setup. A persistent notice shows, there is an off switch, and alerts carry only coarse events.
Escalation: an unfixed item alerts Kevin after 24 hours; the second guardian is told if it is still open 24 hours after that.
Scam coverage: fake virus alerts, scam texts and links, remote-control apps, stalkerware list, daily threat-feed checks.
Her control: she can change guardians and settings any time. Guardians are told when she does.
