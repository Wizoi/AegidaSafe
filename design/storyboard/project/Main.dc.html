HOW TO READ THEM
Phone frames on cream are Aegida's own screens (light theme; dark where shown). Grey frames are other apps or Android's own Settings. Grey-blue frames are a guardian's phone. Each board ends with Built or Planned and the emulator check that verifies it.
Look and feel: teal shield, cream and dark themes, big Atkinson Hyperlegible type, her safety picture in the header. Never red. The app only ever says "No known problems found."
The words on these boards are read from Texts.kt and Problem.kt by design/storyboard/generators/gen_v2.py. See docs/storyboard-status.md for the table and the wording differences from the old storyboard.
