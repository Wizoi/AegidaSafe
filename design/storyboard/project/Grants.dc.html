Rules for this follow-up
Only a guardian who was told about the problem hears that she said it was hers.
Nothing is sent when alerts are switched off.
A plain 'Got it' on a scam warning sends nothing.
The follow-up wording is the same words every time; Aegida cannot add anything else.
