## WP17: a website she allowed (findings #148, Kevin, Oct 15)

Four rounds of word rules (findings #109, #126, #133, #139) tried to tell a fake warning from real news for a site she allowed, and
each round was wrong both ways. The rule now does not guess. For a site she had at setup or said Yes to, a notice that passes the
scare-word gate (`ScareWords.REGEX`, read after look-alike letters are undone) is hidden and she is told; the guardian is never told.
A notice that does not pass the gate is left alone. A site she did not allow is unchanged: alarming words make it a fake warning and
the guardian is told (WP6, WP16). Repeats, snooze, No and the failure states are those of WP1-WP8.

`Problem.SiteScaryNews` (the stored name is kept so rows already in the database still read): she sees "A website you allowed sent
an alarming message. Aegida hid it. Tap to stop that website." (Needs a tap; the tap opens that site's own switch); History says "A
website you allowed sent an alarming message" with the site and its words. It has no guardian sentence, so neither the first note nor
the one-day reminder is sent.
